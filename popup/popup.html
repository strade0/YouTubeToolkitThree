2x Speed Lock

Hold left-click & hover top 2x icon to lock speed

2x
Hover & release to lock or reset

Releasing over the 2x icon locks this speed. Doing it again resets back to 1.0x (Normal).

How it works
  1. Hold Left-Click: Video speeds up to 2x (YouTube built-in).
  2. Hover 2x Icon: Drag cursor up over the 2x pill at top of video.
  3. Let Go: Releases into continuous 2x playback (syncs settings menu).
  4. Reset to 1x: Hold and release over the 2x icon again.
