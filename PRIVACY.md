# Privacy Policy — YouTube Toolkit

Last updated: 23 August 2026

YouTube Toolkit is a Chrome extension that runs only on youtube.com. It is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.

## Data we collect

The extension does **not** send any data to the developer or to any server. There is no analytics, advertising, or account system.

Settings and stats stay in your browser:

- **Feature settings** (on/off, volume sensitivity, layout width, skip delay, 2x speed lock, and similar) are saved with Chrome’s `storage.sync` / `storage.local` so they persist and can sync with your Chrome profile.
- **Volume** is also remembered in YouTube’s own page storage (`localStorage` / `sessionStorage` on youtube.com) so the player volume stays consistent in that tab.
- **SkipIt stats** (jump count, estimated time saved) are stored only in `chrome.storage.local` on your device.

The extension reads the YouTube page only to:

- adjust volume from a drag gesture
- resize the layout
- click YouTube’s native “Jump ahead” control after a single skip
- lock or reset playback speed when you hold-click and release over YouTube’s native 2x control (it does not send speed data anywhere)

It does not read your Google account, watch history, comments, or chat contents for any other purpose.

## Sharing

Nothing is shared with third parties.
